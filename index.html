Prototype notes

Speech synthesis runs on this device. The model and WebAssembly runtime are downloaded from their hosting providers during setup. Model availability can be affected by browser storage eviction. This is a technical test, not a finished audiobook reader.

MediaPipe Tasks may transmit usage/performance metrics under Google's privacy notice; this prototype does not send your test text to a cloud TTS endpoint.
